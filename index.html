How the lines are priced

Spread = home Pwr − away Pwr + the home team’s Massey home-field value (about 2.2 points). Each team’s expected score = its Off rating minus the opponent’s Def rating, plus half the home-field value to the host; the total is the sum. Moneylines use Massey’s win probabilities (51%, 96%, 59%) plus 2.5% per side as the book’s margin, capped so heavy favorites don’t go past about −3200. If you edit a spread, the moneyline reprices from a normal curve with a 17-point standard deviation, which matches Massey’s numbers on the close games. Spreads and totals are −110 both sides.

A parlay multiplies the posted odds of each leg, one leg per game. A same-game parlay (SGP) combines legs from one game: a spread or moneyline, a total, and any player props. These legs are correlated, so the price comes from a joint probability model instead of simple multiplication: the margin and the total share a small positive correlation when the favorite is involved (a favorite covering tends to come with a higher score), which makes favorite-and-over pay less than independent legs and favorite-and-under pay more. Spread and moneyline can’t be combined in one game because one implies the other. An SGPx combines two or more SGPs from different games (games are treated as independent), with extra single legs allowed. SGPs carry a 9% margin. One lost leg loses the whole bet. Use Won or Lost on an open bet to settle it and update the bankroll.

Ottawa player props use each player’s per-game average from the Ottawa stat sheet (games played, not team games), with no opponent or game-script adjustment. Yardage, reception and tackle lines are the average rounded to the nearest half point at −110. Touchdown, field goal, interception and sack props use a Poisson count model with the player’s average as the expected count and a 2.5% margin per side. Same-game props are priced with a correlated model. Ottawa’s scoring is tied negatively to Baker’s margin and positively to the total, and each Ottawa offensive prop is tied to Ottawa’s scoring, so yardage and touchdown overs pair strongly with the Ottawa spread, moneyline and the over. Props for the same player are tied tightly, and Edwards’s passing props are tied to Johnson’s receiving props. Interceptions and sacks move with Ottawa playing well, and tackles move the other way. The joint probability comes from an 80,000-draw simulation with a fixed seed, so the same slip always gets the same price. Each SGP carries the same compounding margin as −110 legs (about 9% for two legs, 13% for three, 17% for four).
